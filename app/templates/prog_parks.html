Nurturing Mysuru's green spaces with community pride

About This Programme

Mysuru's parks are community treasures. We invite corporates and motivated individuals to adopt specific parks — taking responsibility for their upkeep, cleanliness, and beautification.

A direct, visible connection between a sponsor and their neighbourhood park builds lasting pride and ownership that benefits the entire community for generations to come.
