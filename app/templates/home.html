Our Vision

To make Mysuru the cleanest city in India — and to create a model that the rest of India can follow.

Mysuru is a city of heritage, culture, and pride. Lakhs of tourists visit us every year. Our streets, parks, and public spaces are the first impression we give the world. We believe that impression should be one of dignity and cleanliness.

ActionMysuru is not a government programme. It is a citizen movement — a group of people who have decided that waiting is not an option, and that the city we love deserves better.
