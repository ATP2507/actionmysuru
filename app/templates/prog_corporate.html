About This Programme

GRS Fantasy Park has already voluntarily adopted the cleanliness of the road and divider in front of its premises. We want to scale this.

Corporates will commit to adopting the roads, dividers, and public spaces around their premises. A Corporate Cleanliness Award will recognise the best performers and inspire healthy competition across Mysuru's business community.
