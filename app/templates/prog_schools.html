Teaching the next generation of responsible citizens

About This Programme

Schools will adopt the streets and areas around their campuses. Beyond keeping spaces clean, this programme teaches students that social responsibility extends beyond the classroom and the school gate.

The children of Mysuru will grow up knowing the city is theirs to care for — building habits and values that will last a lifetime.
