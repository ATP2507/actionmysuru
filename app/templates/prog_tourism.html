🏛️ Tourism Pilot Project

Making Mysuru's iconic destinations shine for the world

About This Programme

Mysuru's tourist sites are our face to the world. We propose adopting one high-footfall destination — such as the Mysuru Zoo or the Palace precinct — and maintaining it to a world-class standard of cleanliness throughout the day.

We may not know exactly how to do this yet, but once we crack the model at one location, it becomes replicable across every tourist site in Mysuru, and eventually, across India.
